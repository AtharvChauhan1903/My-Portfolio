About Me

Who I Am

My name is Atharv Chauhan, and I am a student who is taking my first steps into the world of software development. I am currently learning programming and web development while exploring different areas of technology.


My Journey Into Programming

I started learning programming because I wanted to understand how software works and eventually create things of my own. I am currently focusing on Java and strengthening my programming fundamentals through practice and problem-solving.

Alongside Java, I have learned the fundamentals of HTML and have started creating webpages and small projects to put what I learn into practice.


What I Am Interested In...

I am particularly interested in software development and web development. I want to understand both the frontend and backend sides of applications rather than limiting myself to only one area.

As I continue learning, I plan to explore technologies such as CSS, JavaScript, React, backend development, databases, and other technologies used in full-stack development.


How I Learn....

I currently use online tutorials and videos to learn new programming concepts. After learning a concept, I try to apply it by solving the challenges provided in the tutorials or creating and attempting challenges of my own.

When I get stuck, I try to understand where I went wrong and work through the problem with guidance. I then test my solution and use what I learned to tackle the next challenge.

My goal is to gradually become more independent at solving problems rather than simply following tutorials.


My Future Goal

My long-term goal is to become a Java Full-Stack Developer and build useful, practical applications. I want to continue improving my programming fundamentals and learn the technologies required to become a well-rounded developer.

Another long-term goal of mine is to build a personal AI assistant capable of helping with everyday tasks, managing information, answering questions, and interacting with different applications. This idea is inspired by assistants such as JARVIS from Iron Man, although I understand that building something like this will require learning many areas of software development and AI.
